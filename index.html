Simplified Rummy: 13 cards per player. The game automatically uses enough 52-card decks for the table size (including multiple decks when needed). Draw one card, then discard one. Sets are 3–4 cards of the same rank; runs are 3+ consecutive cards in the same suit. Aces count 1, face cards 10. Finish is available at 10 or fewer deadwood points.
